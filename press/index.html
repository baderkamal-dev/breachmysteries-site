About the game

Breach is a collection of 150 murder-mystery logic puzzles in the classic deduction-grid style. Each case gives a victim, three to five suspects, their weapons and locations, and a short list of clues. Players mark the grid (✕ to rule out, ● to confirm) and name who did it, what they used and where. Every case is checked by a solver to have exactly one solution, so it never comes down to guessing.

  • Breach Corporate: startups, server rooms and boardrooms.
  • Breach Gulf: a Gulf family business empire, from glass towers and majlis to desert camps.
  • Breach Cybersecurity: inside the cybersecurity industry, at the SOC at 3 a.m.

Four difficulty tiers (Easy to Expert), achievements, a dark interface, Reduced Motion and Differentiate Without Color support.
